# Typography References

---

# Inter

Purpose:
Neutral, highly legible sans-serif. Exceptional weight range (100-900). The most common premium neutral choice for web.

Best For:
- Body text on any product
- UI text and labels
- Dashboard and utility interfaces
- Any project where type should be invisible (functional, not expressive)

Pairing:
- Works alone across all weights as a single-family system
- Pairs with Fraunces or Playfair Display for contrast

Source: Google Fonts, rsms.me/inter

---

# Satoshi

Purpose:
Geometric humanist sans-serif. More personality than Inter with the same functional range. Slightly warmer.

Best For:
- SaaS landing pages that want more character than Inter
- Consumer-facing products
- Projects where Inter feels too neutral

Pairing:
- Works alone across full weight range
- Pairs well with a transitional serif for display use

Source: Fontshare (fontshare.com)

---

# Geist

Purpose:
Clean, modern sans-serif created by Vercel. Includes a mono variant.

Best For:
- Developer-focused products
- Technical documentation
- Products that want to align with modern dev tooling aesthetic

Pairing:
- Geist + Geist Mono for code-integrated designs

Source: vercel.com/font, Google Fonts

---

# Fraunces

Purpose:
Optical size variable serif with unusual character. Strong personality, works large and small.

Best For:
- Display headlines requiring strong visual character
- Editorial and creative products
- Contrast pairing with clean geometric sans

Pairing:
- Fraunces (display) + Inter or Satoshi (body)

Source: Google Fonts

---

# Playfair Display

Purpose:
High-contrast transitional serif. Elegant and editorial at large sizes.

Best For:
- Premium, luxury, or editorial products
- Display headlines where elegance is the signal
- Products differentiating from the tech-sans norm

Risk:
Can look dated if overused with predictable weight choices. Use at extremes (very heavy or very light) for freshness.

Pairing:
- Playfair Display (display) + DM Sans or Inter (body)

Source: Google Fonts

---

# Space Grotesk

Purpose:
Geometric sans-serif with technical personality. Uppercase letters have distinctive character.

Best For:
- Developer tools
- Technical SaaS with personality
- Products where "technical but human" is the positioning

Pairing:
- Works alone or with Space Mono for code sections

Source: Google Fonts

---

# DM Sans

Purpose:
Clean, slightly warm sans-serif. More approachable than Inter with less personality than Satoshi.

Best For:
- Consumer products
- Health, wellness, education
- Products targeting non-technical audiences

Source: Google Fonts

---

# Cabinet Grotesk

Purpose:
Wide, editorial grotesque. Strong character at large sizes. Unusual proportions that create visual tension.

Best For:
- Display headlines requiring a distinctive look
- Creative agencies
- Products where the type itself is a visual element

Pairing:
- Cabinet Grotesk (display) + Satoshi or Inter (body)

Source: Fontshare (fontshare.com)

---

# Recommended Pairings Reference

Technical-premium: Geist + Geist Mono
Clean SaaS: Inter alone (full weight range)
Editorial SaaS: Fraunces (display) + Inter (body)
Warm consumer: DM Sans + Satoshi
Creative/agency: Cabinet Grotesk (display) + Inter (body)
Developer: Space Grotesk + Space Mono
Luxury/premium: Playfair Display (display) + DM Sans (body)

---

# Type Scale Reference

Display: 80-120px, weight 700-900
H1: 56-72px, weight 700-800
H2: 36-48px, weight 600-700
H3: 24-32px, weight 600
H4: 20-24px, weight 600
Body large: 18-20px, weight 400
Body: 15-17px, weight 400
Small/caption: 13-14px, weight 400-500
Label/tag: 11-13px, weight 500-600, letter-spacing 0.08em

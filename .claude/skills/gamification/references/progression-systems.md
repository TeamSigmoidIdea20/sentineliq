# Progression Systems

---

# XP Systems

Purpose:
Reward repeated activity.

Best For:
- learning
- gaming
- onboarding

Risks:
- meaningless grinding
- fake progress

---

# Mastery Trees

Purpose:
Visualize capability growth.

Best For:
- education
- skill tools
- creative software

Strength:
Clear sense of evolution.

---

# Rank Tiers

Purpose:
Segment users by performance.

Examples:
- Bronze
- Silver
- Gold

Best For:
- competitive systems

Risk:
Can discourage weaker users.

---

# Unlock Progression

Purpose:
Reveal features gradually.

Best For:
- reducing overwhelm
- onboarding

Risk:
Artificial restrictions frustrate users.

---

# Prestige Systems

Purpose:
Extend progression after max level.

Best For:
- long-term engagement

Risk:
Infinite grind fatigue.

---

# Challenge Progression

Purpose:
Increase difficulty over time.

Best For:
- fitness
- learning
- games

Strength:
Maintains growth momentum.

---

# Skill Evolution Systems

Purpose:
Reflect actual user improvement.

Best For:
- fitness
- education
- coding

Strongest Long-Term Mechanic.

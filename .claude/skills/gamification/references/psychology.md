# Behavioral Psychology

---

# Intrinsic Motivation

Definition:
Motivation driven by internal satisfaction.

Drivers:
- mastery
- autonomy
- purpose
- curiosity

Long-Term Impact:
Most sustainable form of engagement.

---

# Extrinsic Motivation

Definition:
Motivation driven by external rewards.

Examples:
- points
- badges
- money
- status

Risk:
Can weaken intrinsic motivation if overused.

---

# Self-Determination Theory

Core Human Needs:
- competence
- autonomy
- relatedness

Strong products satisfy all three.

---

# Zeigarnik Effect

People remember unfinished tasks more than completed ones.

Applications:
- progress bars
- incomplete onboarding
- collection systems

Risk:
Can create stress if overused.

---

# Completion Drive

Humans naturally seek closure.

Applications:
- rings
- checklists
- completion meters

Best Practice:
Completion should feel satisfying, not mandatory.

---

# Variable Reward Theory

Uncertain reward magnitude creates anticipation.

Examples:
- card packs
- mystery rewards
- randomized bonuses

Risk:
Can become exploitative.

---

# Loss Aversion

People fear losing more than they enjoy gaining.

Applications:
- streaks
- rank decay
- limited rewards

Risk:
Creates anxiety-driven engagement.

---

# Competence Feedback

People stay engaged when they feel improvement.

Applications:
- personal bests
- rankings
- analytics
- skill milestones

Critical For:
Long-term retention.

---

# Social Proof

People imitate behavior validated by others.

Applications:
- likes
- comments
- reviews
- public activity

Risk:
Can create shallow performative behavior.

---

# Habit Formation

Habit Loop:
- trigger
- action
- reward
- reinforcement

Strong habits:
- reduce friction
- provide immediate feedback
- feel rewarding

---

# Cognitive Load

Too many mechanics reduce clarity and motivation.

Symptoms:
- confusion
- fatigue
- abandonment

Solution:
Keep systems minimal and focused.

---

# Anticipation

Excitement about future rewards drives engagement.

Applications:
- unlock systems
- previews
- upcoming rewards

Often stronger than the reward itself.

---

# Autonomy

Users engage more when they feel in control.

Applications:
- adjustable goals
- optional challenges
- personalized progression

Avoid:
- forced engagement
- rigid systems

---

# Meaningful Progress

Visible progress increases motivation.

Applications:
- leveling
- milestones
- skill evolution

Important:
Progress must feel earned.

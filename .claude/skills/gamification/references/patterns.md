# Gamification Patterns

---

# Completion Loop

Goal:
Increase task completion momentum.

Mechanics:
- progress bars
- segmented onboarding
- completion rings
- daily goals
- milestone indicators

Best For:
- onboarding
- learning apps
- productivity apps
- fitness apps

Risks:
- artificial padding
- fatigue from excessive micro-steps

---

# Competence Loop

Goal:
Make users feel improvement and mastery.

Mechanics:
- skill ratings
- personal bests
- performance analytics
- mastery tiers
- advanced unlocks

Best For:
- education
- fitness
- coding
- gaming
- creative tools

Risks:
- discouraging beginners
- over-complex analytics

---

# Variable Reward Loop

Goal:
Create anticipation and curiosity.

Mechanics:
- randomized rewards
- mystery unlocks
- surprise bonuses
- rotating content
- evolving discoveries

Best For:
- collectible systems
- social products
- entertainment apps

Risks:
- addiction patterns
- gambling-style exploitation

---

# Social Reinforcement Loop

Goal:
Increase consistency through social validation.

Mechanics:
- likes
- kudos
- accountability groups
- cooperative goals
- social streaks

Best For:
- fitness
- communities
- creator platforms

Risks:
- vanity behavior
- shallow validation seeking

---

# Progression Ladder

Goal:
Create long-term growth journey.

Mechanics:
- levels
- rank tiers
- unlockable features
- evolving capabilities

Best For:
- long-term engagement products

Risks:
- meaningless XP inflation
- fake advancement

---

# Local Competition Loop

Goal:
Make competition feel achievable.

Mechanics:
- friend leaderboards
- local rankings
- cohort competition
- seasonal groups

Best For:
- fitness
- education
- creator communities

Risks:
- toxic competitiveness
- discouragement if pools are too large

---

# Collection Loop

Goal:
Drive repeat engagement through completion desire.

Mechanics:
- collectible sets
- achievement collections
- card systems
- cosmetic unlocks

Best For:
- gaming
- entertainment
- fandom products

Risks:
- repetitive grind
- artificial scarcity abuse

---

# Streak Loop

Goal:
Encourage consistency.

Mechanics:
- consecutive day counters
- weekly consistency metrics
- habit chains

Best For:
- habit apps
- language learning
- journaling

Risks:
- anxiety
- guilt
- burnout
- obligation-based engagement

Safe Design:
- streak freezes
- recovery systems
- flexible targets

---

# Onboarding Momentum Loop

Goal:
Get users to first success quickly.

Mechanics:
- guided setup
- instant wins
- starter missions
- progress onboarding

Best For:
- SaaS
- productivity tools
- marketplaces

Risks:
- overcomplicated onboarding
- tutorial fatigue

---

# Mastery Unlock Loop

Goal:
Reward skill growth.

Mechanics:
- advanced tools
- higher difficulty modes
- capability expansion

Best For:
- creative tools
- education
- gaming

Risks:
- gatekeeping

# Reward Systems

---

# Fixed Rewards

Definition:
Predictable rewards.

Examples:
- daily login bonuses
- guaranteed XP

Pros:
- clarity
- reliability

Cons:
- lower excitement

---

# Variable Rewards

Definition:
Reward magnitude varies.

Examples:
- loot systems
- random bonuses

Pros:
- anticipation
- excitement

Cons:
- addiction risk

---

# Social Rewards

Definition:
Recognition from others.

Examples:
- likes
- comments
- badges
- public achievements

Pros:
- belonging
- motivation

Cons:
- vanity optimization

---

# Competence Rewards

Definition:
Rewards tied to improvement.

Examples:
- personal records
- rankings
- mastery unlocks

Strongest long-term retention driver.

---

# Cosmetic Rewards

Definition:
Visual personalization rewards.

Examples:
- skins
- themes
- profile customization

Pros:
- identity expression

Cons:
- low meaning without emotional attachment

---

# Unlock Rewards

Definition:
New capabilities become available.

Examples:
- advanced features
- new tools
- hidden content

Pros:
- curiosity
- progression

---

# Narrative Rewards

Definition:
Story progression rewards.

Examples:
- chapters
- evolving lore
- character progression

Pros:
- emotional investment

---

# Collection Rewards

Definition:
Completion-based rewards.

Examples:
- sets
- collectibles
- achievements

Pros:
- strong completion drive

Cons:
- repetitive grinding

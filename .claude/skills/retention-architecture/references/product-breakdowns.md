# Product Breakdowns

---

# Duolingo

Core Retention System:
Consistency + streak preservation + visible progress.

Primary Drivers:
- habit continuity
- streak identity
- progression visibility
- daily momentum

Strongest Mechanic:
Streak systems combined with lightweight daily effort.

Why It Works:
Low friction allows habits to persist.

Psychological Drivers:
- completion psychology
- consistency identity
- loss aversion
- visible progress

Risks:
- streak anxiety
- learning optimization sacrificed for streak maintenance

Key Lesson:
Small consistent engagement scales better than intense effort.
